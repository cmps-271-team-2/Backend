# Post Moderation Guidelines

These are draft guidelines for user-generated posts.

Allow:
- Honest opinions, reviews, and experiences about courses, cafeterias, campus life, and student life.
- Critical feedback when it is about behavior, quality, or experience and does not target protected characteristics.
- Casual slang, mild frustration, and non-directed profanity when it is not abusive or threatening.

Disallow:
- Threats of violence, encouragement of self-harm, or instructions for illegal or dangerous acts.
- Harassment, bullying, humiliation, or targeted abuse toward a person or group.
- Hate speech or degrading content targeting protected characteristics.
- Sexual exploitation, coercion, or explicit sexual content.
- Doxxing, private personal information, or attempts to identify or expose someone.
- Defamation-style accusations presented as fact without context, especially criminal accusations about identifiable people.
- Spam, scams, phishing, impersonation, or manipulative promotional content. They are not allowed.
- Random text is not allowed.

Needs Review:
- Ambiguous accusations against a named person.
- Content that may be sarcastic, quoted, or missing context.
- Strong insults directed at a specific person but without an explicit threat.
- Posts mentioning drugs, self-harm, violence, or sexual content in a non-obvious context.

Moderation Principles:
- Prefer protecting users from targeted harm over maximizing recall for borderline content.
- Do not reject content solely for negative sentiment.
- Evaluate the full post content and any provided metadata.
- Return concise reasons and name the violated guideline categories when relevant.

Example:

"oerijtgijwpigj" is not allowed.

"This course suckksss" is allowed.

"fucfk fucjkcufckfuk fuckkk i hate professor X fuckkkkk" is not allowed.

"professor X is fuckign annoying" is allowed.
